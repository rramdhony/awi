Use Cases

Real scenarios.
Real outcomes.

East Africa, Kenya & Tanzania

Entering without setting up entities

A company needs to hire quickly across Kenya and Tanzania. Setting up legal entities is too slow. They need workforce in market, compliantly, within weeks.

We assess EOR versus entity, design the workforce model, navigate local employment frameworks and define a compliant onboarding plan.

  • Fast market entry, workforce deployed in weeks
  • Full regulatory compliance from day one
  • A scalable structure ready to grow with you
West Africa, Nigeria

Controlled entry into a complex market

Nigeria is a priority market, but perceived risk is creating board-level hesitation. The company needs a clear-eyed risk assessment and a structured entry path.

We map legal and tax exposure, identify key operational risks, quantify them and design a structured entry model that gives the board a defensible decision.

  • Risk mapped, quantified and mitigated
  • A clear board-ready recommendation
  • Controlled expansion with defined guardrails
Multi-Country, 3 to 5 Markets

Scaling across Africa with consistency

A company has proven one market and now wants to scale across three to five countries. Doing it without a system creates chaos, compliance gaps and inconsistent operations.

We prioritise markets, standardise the workforce model, build the rollout playbook and ensure each market launches to the same operational standard.

  • Faster scaling with less operational friction
  • Consistent compliance standards across markets
  • A system that scales, not just a plan
